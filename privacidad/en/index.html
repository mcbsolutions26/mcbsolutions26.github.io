Privacy policy

Version 1.1 · 10 October 2026

This policy explains what data the apps PlanLine, MultiLine and UniLine (beta version) and this website store and send. It is written in short sentences on purpose.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Marc del Campo Bernal, who uses the trade name MCBSolutions. You can write to us at mcbsolutions@outlook.es.

2. Who this is for

The apps are tools for drawing electrical diagrams. Students and teachers use them. Minors may use them with permission from their family or teacher. Sending feedback is always voluntary. If you are a minor, you do not need to write your e-mail.

3. What is stored on your computer

The apps store some data on your own computer, in the app’s local storage. We do not see it. It does not leave your computer.

  • Your project, with autosave, so your work is not lost.
  • Your preferences, such as the language.
  • An error log with the last 50 errors. The texts of your projects are removed from that log before it is saved.
  • Up to 10 reports that you chose to save and send later.
  • A beta control item: the most recent day the app was opened and the version whose notice you hid.
  • The project files that you save on your computer. Those files are yours.

If you uninstall the app or clear the browser data, this data is lost. Save your projects to a file.

4. What data leaves your computer and when

Nothing leaves your computer while you work. Data leaves only if you press “Send” in “Suggestions / Report a problem”. If you choose “Open in e-mail”, your e-mail program opens and you decide whether to send it.

5. Who receives the data

We use the Web3Forms service to receive feedback. Web3Forms acts as a processor. According to its published texts, it operates from India and uses Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner as providers. To filter spam it uses CleanTalk and Akismet, which may receive the sender’s IP and e-mail. Transfers outside the European Economic Area may happen. Web3Forms says it relies on standard contractual clauses. Checked on 9 October 2026.

If you use “Open in e-mail”, the message goes through your e-mail provider and through Outlook, which is the provider of our mailbox.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use user accounts.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising in the apps.
  • We do not sell data.
  • We do not send your project unless you tick the box.
  • We do not build profiles or take automated decisions.

7. Why we use the data and on what basis

We use feedback to reply to you, fix errors and improve the apps. The basis is your consent when you press “Send”. For the contact e-mail and the project, it is also your choice to tick them.

8. How long we keep the data

We keep feedback for as long as needed to handle it. Then we delete it. Web3Forms states that it keeps submissions for three years at most from the submission date and then deletes them. This is the provider’s maximum period, not a commitment of ours. You can ask us to delete it earlier.

9. Your rights

You can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data. Write to us at mcbsolutions@outlook.es. Give the error code or the sending date so that we can find it. You can complain to the data protection authority of your country. In Spain it is the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

10. This website

This website is static. It uses no cookies, scripts, analytics, external fonts or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log technical data about visits, such as the IP. We do not control that log.

11. Minors

We do not require minors to give data. If a minor sends feedback that contains personal data, their family or teacher can ask us to delete it. Write to us at mcbsolutions@outlook.es.

12. Changes and contact

If we change what data the apps collect, we will update this policy and its date. This policy is not a substitute for legal advice. Contact: mcbsolutions@outlook.es.
